Take-Two Interactive (TTWO): GTA VI Goes on Sale Next Week; the Business Behind It Has Not Started – Initiate at Buy

By David Bratucu

We initiate coverage of Take-Two Interactive at Buy with a 12-month price target of $290. The report works backward from the share price and weighs four scenarios for GTA VI Online.
