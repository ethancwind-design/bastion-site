Our Funds.

Two student-run funds with distinct approaches: Locust Partners, our first $75K fund, and a new $45K fund allocated to technology and the S&P 500.

Fund 01 · Locust Partners

Our First Fund.
$75K.

Locust Partners invests across a broad set of asset classes and approaches. Four strategy committees evaluate long and short opportunities in equities, credit and convertibles, global quantitative strategies, and venture capital through alternative data, portfolio construction, and risk-aware underwriting.

Strategy Committees

Portfolio Oversight

Investment Committee

Locust Partners.
Multiple perspectives.

Portfolio managers lead research within each strategy, combining company fundamentals, alternative data, and market signals to evaluate opportunities across asset classes.

The investment committee brings those perspectives together through position sizing, liquidity review, and risk oversight. The fund is run by students as part of the Bastion Investment Society.

Fund 02 · Meridian Fund (working name)

Our Second Fund.
$45K.

The Meridian Fund is a working name for our new $45K fund. Its allocation is 75% technology and 25% S&P 500.
