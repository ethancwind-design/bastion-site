The Fund

Locust Partners

$115K

Locust Partners is Bastion's long-only equity fund, built on fundamental company research and long-term conviction.

The fund’s capital will be finalized after the release of our first quarterly research report, and the strongest pitches from that report will be incorporated into the portfolio by May 2027, at the close of the spring semester.

“Long-only, built on conviction.”

Locust Partners holds long-only equity positions built on fundamental company research and long-term conviction.

“Ideas from every committee.”

Each committee researches the companies in its coverage. The strongest ideas from the quarterly report are considered for the fund.

“Sized for up to twelve months.”

Positions are sized proportionately for a holding period of up to 12 months and tracked through the year.

“Reviewed by the Investment Committee.”

The Investment Committee reviews every position, checks the figures and sources, and decides what goes into the fund.
