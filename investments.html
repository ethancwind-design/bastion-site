      Locust Partners
      Equity Fund

      Locust Partners Equity Fund is a concentrated long-only equity portfolio built around a small number of high-conviction ideas across technology, consumer and health care.

      We favor durable businesses, size positions by the strength of the thesis, and stay disciplined on risk. The fund is run by students at the University of Pennsylvania as part of the Bastion Investment Society.
