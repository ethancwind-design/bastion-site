Team Leadership

Ethan WindUniversity of Pennsylvania, B.S. Economics (Finance)

Ethan Wind studies Finance at the University of Pennsylvania and was selected as the Fall 2026 Wall Street Guide Fellowship Competition winner from more than 560 students, earning a full scholarship valued at $19,900. He is close friends with Luke Pierce, who also graduated from Sanford H. Calhoun High School.

This summer he was an Equity Research Intern at AlphaStone Equities in London, where he built a fully integrated three-statement model for BMW and a bottom-up revenue model for NVIDIA, and performed earnings quality analysis under a former head of European equity research at Barclays Capital.

In high school Ethan founded Project Shield, a free financial literacy program that has taught more than 150 students on Long Island, and won first place in New York State in DECA's Financial Literacy Project. He also taught financial literacy in Spanish in Granada, Nicaragua, with Empowerment International.

Arna RakhimbergenovaUniversity of Pennsylvania, B.S. Economics (Finance and Business Economics & Public Policy)

Arna Rakhimbergenova studies Finance and Business Economics and Public Policy at the University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School. She is a member of the Wharton Investment and Trading Group, an Associate Consultant at Penn International Impact Consulting on Project HEAVEN in Fiji, and a Fellow of Pi Lambda Sigma.

Before Penn, she interned with UNICEF in Kazakhstan, coordinating a nationwide network of more than 200 youth volunteers and earning an honorary mention from UNICEF Kazakhstan's representative. She also researched how global civil service standards apply in developing economies, comparing Kazakhstan's public service with South Korea's.

Arna was elected president of the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools student government with 1,122 votes and led a charity ball that raised $15,000 in sponsorships and ticket sales. She won first place at the AstanaHub Republican Pitch for Finmate, a financial literacy startup, and graduated with the Honorary Red Diploma.
