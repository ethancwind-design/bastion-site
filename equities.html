Team Leadership

Srinidith GummadiCornell University, Dyson School of Applied Economics and Business Management

Srinidith Gummadi studies Economics at Cornell University in the Dyson School of Applied Economics and Business Management. At Jericho High School he prioritized business and economics, served as captain of the varsity basketball team, and co-founded the Brighter Futures Foundation.

Since 2023 he has worked as Supply Chain Manager at 59th Street Pharmacy in Brooklyn, running inbound orders, inventory, and replenishment. In the summer of 2025 he joined the Harvard Undergraduate Ventures Tech Summer Program as an intern at ScoutOut.ai, an AI startup that matches contractors with planners, and wrote a proposal for the company to expand into Charlotte based on its tech gap, rapid growth, and sustainability.

He plans to major in Economics or Real Estate and brings that interest in how businesses and markets are built to Bastion's equities team.

Asher WanUniversity of Pennsylvania, B.S. Economics

Asher Wan studies Economics at the University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School, with an intended concentration in Finance and Statistics. He is involved in the Wharton Asia Exchange, the Wharton China Association, the Wharton Undergraduate Statistics Society, and Penn Club Swim.

He has competed at the top of international economics and finance competitions: National Champion and International Gold Medalist at the International Finance Olympiad (top 6 of 283), champion of the International Business Olympiad (1st of 683), and a National Finalist at the USA Economics Olympiad (9th of more than 1,100). He is also a National Merit Finalist and a University of Pennsylvania Book Award recipient.

Asher worked as an Investment Intern at 215 Capital in Philadelphia, doing commercial due diligence on biotech companies, and is a Fellow of The Germination Project, a leadership fellowship for Philadelphia's next generation. At The Episcopal Academy he presided over the Economics Club and FBLA and captained varsity swimming.
