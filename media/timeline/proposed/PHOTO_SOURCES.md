## Rights and attribution

- Creative Commons entries require the photographer credit and license link shown above. ShareAlike requirements apply to adapted versions of those photos.
- Carol M. Highsmith photographs are [public domain according to the Library of Congress](https://wwws.loc.gov/rr/print/res/482_high.html). The Library of Congress record is linked for each item.
- The historic Library of Congress items are described by their individual records as having no known restrictions on publication. Their item records remain the source of that rights description.
- The 1915 Widener photograph is marked public domain by its [Wikimedia Commons record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HarvardUniversity_WidenerLibrary_Reading_c1915.jpg); the image author is unknown.
- This proposal records image permissions and attribution. It does not imply any affiliation with or endorsement by the photographed institutions.

## Curation checks

- The 28 photos are each assigned to one active timeline photo slot. The preserved report, artwork, magazine cover, and ARGOS media are separate assets.
- No proposed image file matches an existing Bastion media asset by file hash. A visual pass and perceptual-hash scan against 106 existing raster assets found no apparent reuse.
- Sources favor campus architecture, academic work, print production, archive work, and capital markets. No handshake or staged corporate stock photo is included.
