What We Do

Bastion is officially launching in spring 2027 with an internal goal, an external goal, and a community goal.

Internal Goal.The Curriculum.

The Society's internal goal is to redefine the investment process and to aggregate that knowledge into a single quarterly report.

January

Each chapter recruits new analysts at its school through applications and interviews.

Recruiting runs across all eight Ivy League campuses at the same time.

EducationThe Curriculum

New members complete the internal curriculum. We give members a list of valuable materials and guide them through sessions led by senior members.

March

Every member joins a committee and does deep research on one or two ideas within its coverage. Analysts begin building models for the companies they cover.

We focus on alternative data and new perspectives in our research, using our partnership with ARGOS ATLAS.

ReviewInvestment Committee

The MagazineEvery member’s best work.

Every member who submits high-quality work is included in the quarterly report, designed in Canva. The design amplifies work students already do for their investment club stock pitches and virtual internships, instead of taking time away from those commitments.

External Goal.Partnership Value.

Bastion works with partner firms, including investment funds, to take on their hardest research questions. In return, members learn directly from industry professionals through mentorship, fund visits, and treks.

“Engaged from the first meeting to the final deliverable.”

Bastion students stay highly engaged throughout a project, meet every deliverable deadline, and bring thoughtful evidence and questions. We work closely with each partner to help shape its investment objective as it evolves.

“Professionalism and analytical rigor.”

Across the Ivy League, we combine professionalism with deep analytical rigor. We uncover key insights that can strengthen a thesis, and we give investment recommendations and sizing that are clear, actionable, and aligned with your goals.

“Learning from people who do the work.”

Bastion students value mentors at partner firms and bring energy to every conversation about research, valuation, and careers.

“Seeing the day-to-day in person.”

Bastion members are grateful for the chance to learn in person at our partner funds and see the day-to-day work of investment professionals.

ARGOS ATLAS

Collaborating with ARGOS ATLAS

The Bastion Investment Society teaches members in all four strategy groups to use artificial intelligence to find trends in alternative data.

We teach analysts to build machine learning software that finds trends in more than 20,000 ships at sea, and to see how daily port arrivals, departures, and congestion affect stock prices. We also teach them to track roughly 14,000 active aircraft to understand company inventories and whether a company's aviation operations are accelerating or decelerating.

We build monitoring systems that put more than 200,000 active cameras to work, giving analysts earlier notice of geolocated conflicts, wars, and natural disasters. We track foot traffic and water levels at critical energy infrastructure, and we learn to extract and analyze data on more than 35,000 global power facilities (nuclear, solar, wind, hydro, and gas), plus more than 4,000 data centers, telecommunications hubs, and undersea fiber-optic cable routes.
