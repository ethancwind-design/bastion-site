Daniel AdibiHarvard University, A.B. Physics

Daniel Adibi studies Physics at Harvard University and is an Analyst for Harvard Undergraduate Capital Partners. He has spent years capturing images of space, and his photograph of the Virgo Galaxy Cluster was featured as NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day on April 17, 2025.

He taught himself astrophotography over two years. For the Virgo image he traveled to the dark skies near Franklin, West Virginia, and tracked the target through the night, stacking multiple five-minute exposures into about six hours of data that he later processed into the final image.

At The Episcopal Academy, Daniel ranked first in his class. He received the Phi Beta Kappa Award for Highest Scholarship, the Alumni Prize for Highest Standing in Mathematics, and prizes for the highest grades in physics, chemistry, and biology. He is also an AIME qualifier and a National Merit Finalist.

Luke PiercePrinceton University, B.S.E. Electrical and Computer Engineering

Luke Pierce is an incoming first-year at Princeton University studying Electrical and Computer Engineering. He graduated as valedictorian of Sanford H. Calhoun High School, ranked first in a class of 307, and is a National Merit Commended Scholar and AP Capstone Diploma recipient.

Since 2024 he has been a Visiting Scholar at The Feinstein Institutes, where he worked with a team of more than ten researchers and used multivariable logistic regression and jackknife resampling in R to analyze clinical data. The resulting poster won an Abstract Award at the 2025 Society for Maternal-Fetal Medicine Pregnancy Meeting.

Luke founded the Aegis Health Foundation, a nonprofit that has managed more than $3,000 in proceeds, and received a Citation for Community Service from the Nassau County Legislature. He is close friends with Ethan Wind, who attended Sanford H. Calhoun High School with him.
